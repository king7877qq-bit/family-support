We are more than a list of needs.

We are people, a home, and the little things that make life feel whole.

“
We do not dream of a perfect life… only a morning when we do not worry about how to meet our family's needs.
From our family, with honesty and gratitude.

We are doing our best to hold on, even when days are hard.

In Syria, life's essentials have become a daily burden for many families. Ours is one of them. We do what work we can and stretch every resource, but irregular income and rising living costs make food, medicine, household bills and heating a constant challenge.

That is why we created this small family initiative. We share our story as it is, hoping to reach people who can stand beside us, even in a small way.

♡With gratitude, from our family to you
